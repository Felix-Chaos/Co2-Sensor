# CO2 Sensor TTGO 45-Degree Desktop Stand

A modified 45-degree desktop stand and case designed for the **LilyGO TTGO T-Display ESP32** and the **Sensirion SCD30** CO2 sensor. 

This case houses the electronics perfectly while angling the display at a 45-degree angle for easy reading on a desk or nightstand.

### 🌟 Features
* **Perfect Viewing Angle:** 45-degree tilt makes the display easy to read while sitting at a desk.
* **Optimized Ventilation:** Features an open back/grid design specifically to allow maximum airflow to the SCD30 sensor. This is critical to prevent the heat from the ESP32 from skewing the temperature and humidity readings.
* **Button Access:** Designed to allow easy access to the physical buttons on the T-Display board.
* **USB-C Port:** Clean cutout for powering the device.

### 🛠️ Hardware Requirements
* 1x LilyGO TTGO T-Display (ESP32)
* 1x Sensirion SCD30 Sensor Module
* Jumper wires for I2C and power

*Note: This case is meant to accompany the [CO2 Sensor TTGO Custom Dashboard](https://github.com/Felix-Chaos/Co2-Sensor) project, which integrates these sensors seamlessly with Home Assistant via MQTT.*

### 🖨️ Printing Instructions
* **Material:** PLA, PETG, or ABS
* **Infill:** 15-20%
* **Supports:** Depending on your printer's bridging capabilities, you may need minor supports for the USB cutout or the display window overhang.
* **Orientation:** Print the main body flat on its back or bottom, and the front panel face-down on the build plate.
